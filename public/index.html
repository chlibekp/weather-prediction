    How it works

    For each place we download two years of hourly temperature, humidity, pressure, wind and precipitation from the Open-Meteo archive. A seasonal mean, built from yearly and daily cycles and their interaction, captures what is normal for each hour of each season.

    What is left over is modeled with a seasonal ARIMA per variable. The candidate orders are screened by Hannan–Rissanen regression and refined by least squares, and the best one is picked by AIC. Differencing is chosen on a two-week validation window, which also calibrates the ranges.

    Rain chance comes from a logistic regression on the forecast humidity, pressure tendency, wind and temperature anomaly, plus whether it was raining when the forecast was made. Every number in the section above is measured on the last 30 days, which the model never saw during training.
